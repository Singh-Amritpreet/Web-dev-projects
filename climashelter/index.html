Model notes. This console runs a simplified single-zone, lumped-capacitance heat-balance model: one indoor air-and-mass node exchanging heat with the outside through the walls, roof and glazing, plus ventilation and solar gain through windows. It does not account for ground coupling, shading geometry, occupant loads, or moisture transport. The backup energy figure assumes an idealised thermostat that exactly holds the comfort band — a proxy for the heating or cooling load a mechanical system would otherwise need to cover. Treat results as directional, for comparing design choices against each other and against a reference construction, not as a certified energy calculation.
